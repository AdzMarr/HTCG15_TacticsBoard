Recording

📱 Use your phone's built-in screen recorder

In-browser screen recording isn't supported on Android or iPhone browsers. To record this drill with sound:

  1. Swipe down from the top of your screen to open Quick Settings.
  2. Tap Screen Record (add it to Quick Settings first if you don't see it).
  3. Enable microphone audio when prompted, then start recording.
  4. Return to this page and run through your drill.
  5. Stop the recording from Quick Settings or the notification shade.
